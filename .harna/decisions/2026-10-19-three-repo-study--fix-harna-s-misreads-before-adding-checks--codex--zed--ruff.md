# Three-repo study: fix harna's misreads before adding checks (codex, zed, ruff)

Mapping codex (2.09M lines, Bazel root), zed (1.78M, 258 crates) and ruff (840k) with harna showed that at this scale harna's reading was the weak point, not its check catalog: nested workspaces, use-statement parsing, name shadowing, crate-root naming conventions, shared package names and quadratic lookups produced thousands of false findings and 2-3 minute validates. Those were fixed in the study burst (unreleased, 0.3.47) with a regression test per repo; new checks (R1-R11) are scheduled after the remaining misreads (H9 macro-emitted code, H10 module-tree reachability, H11 init's AGENTS.md edits). Kept: two Haiku readers total, reused across repos with their deno context. Rejected: a process-wide crate-index cache (stale across tests that rewrite Cargo.toml); validate now loads the index ~12 times, ~2s on codex — acceptable.
